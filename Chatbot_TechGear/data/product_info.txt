FAQs by Category:

SmartWatch FAQs:
Q1: How long does the battery last?
A1: Battery life depends on usage. With screen always-on and continuous monitoring, expect 3-4 days. With moderate use, 5-7 days is typical.

Q2: Can I swim with my SmartWatch?
A2: Water resistance depends on model. Pro X is 5ATM (50m), suitable for snorkeling but not diving. Pro Max is 10ATM (100m), suitable for swimming. Essential is 3ATM, splash resistant only.

Q3: Is the watch compatible with both iOS and Android?
A3: Yes, all our SmartWatch models work with both iOS 12+ and Android 6.0+. Some advanced features may vary slightly by platform.

Q4: Can I replace the battery myself?
A4: No, battery replacement should only be done by authorized service centers. Unauthorized opening voids warranty.

Q5: What should I do if my watch freezes?
A5: Try force restart: Hold the power button for 10 seconds until you see the restart animation. If issue persists, contact support.

Wireless Earbuds FAQs:
Q1: How do I connect my earbuds to my device?
A1: Open the case, enable Bluetooth on your device, select the earbud model from available devices, and confirm pairing.

Q2: How do I turn on ANC?
A2: Most models: Press and hold the right earbud for 2 seconds. Check your manual for specific model instructions.

Q3: Can I use just one earbud?
A3: Yes, all models support single earbud use. Simply use one earbud while the other charges in the case.

Q4: Why is the battery draining faster than usual?
A4: Battery drain increases with: ANC enabled, high volume, continuous connectivity, and cold temperatures. Try disabling ANC or lowering volume.

Q5: Are the earbuds waterproof?
A5: They are water-resistant but not waterproof. IPX4 rated models are splash resistant. IPX5-7 models are sweat and splash resistant. Avoid submersion beyond rating.

Power Bank FAQs:
Q1: How many times can I charge my phone?
A1: Approximately 4-5 times for 20000mAh power bank with a typical 4000mAh phone. Actual times vary with phone capacity and charging efficiency.

Q2: Is it safe to leave the power bank plugged in overnight?
A2: Yes, all TechGear power banks have overcharge protection. They'll stop charging once full, but we recommend unplugging for optimal battery health.

Q3: Can I use the power bank while it's charging?
A3: Yes, you can charge multiple devices while the power bank itself is charging (pass-through charging).

Q4: Why is the power bank getting warm?
A4: Slight warmth is normal during fast charging. If it gets hot (too hot to touch), disconnect immediately and contact support.

Q5: What's the difference between 22.5W and 65W charging?
A5: Higher wattage = faster charging. 65W can fully charge a laptop in 3 hours, while 22.5W is for phones and tablets (typically 1.5-2 hours).
